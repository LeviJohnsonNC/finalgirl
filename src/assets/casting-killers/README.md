# Killer casting photographs

These 768 × 1152 WebPs are portrait renditions for the killer picker, selected slot and dossier. They take precedence only when the selected source is the corresponding photographic identity, including the existing fallback for a missing official portrait. Opening references, Play stages and banners, source identities, and the Collection shelf keep their own sources. The six approved casting portraits retain their original assets and framing.

Built-in image generation used the existing identity for likeness and the approved Inkanyamba snapshot for ordinary 35mm light and materials. Poltergeist also used the tangible performer from the Play header. The chosen original outputs were resized without stretching and encoded as WebP, with a 180 KiB maximum per portrait.
