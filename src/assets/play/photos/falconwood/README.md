# Falconwood Play photography

Original candid 1980s 35mm-style images. The [manifest](manifest.json) records source identifiers, scene direction, identity references, digests, dimensions, focal positions and byte counts. Each of the 52 stills has a distinct source.

The shallow Falconwood owner banner uses a crop of the river panorama. Mission heroes cover the police station, mall, lab, library and Hunt, with separate success photographs for all five missions and an intermediate active Hunt photograph. The Best Friend has a prone still, and all five Friends and two Event people have separate identity-matched present, saved and killed stills. Left play dims the present image. The river and delivery van have separate visible state scenes. Ordinary Terror stills have 400px WebP derivatives for the death cause tiles. The conditional Hunt Terror uses the waiting Hunt image.

The images are illustrative. They establish no board position, damage, item use, casualty or progress.
