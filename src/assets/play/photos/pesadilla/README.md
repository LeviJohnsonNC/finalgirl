# Pesadilla Verde Play photographs

The built-in image generator produced the original snapshots in this set. The same camera era, natural exposure and damp rainforest treatment carry across the images. The original generation filenames are recorded in the manifest. Runtime WebP files are bounded at 960 × 640 and 160 KiB, without enlargement. The location header is a crop of the existing Collection scene.

- **Huey.** An olive utility helicopter in a damp clearing. The away photograph uses its original camera position, bent grass and the same three palms on the ridge. An unknown state uses the jungle header.
- **Soldiers.** The same anonymous three-person patrol on a muddy trail. Watchful, Twitchy and composed poses respond to the recorded friendly-fire rules.
- **River and weather.** Lower, Central and Upper River each have their own piranhas photograph. The rain and Rivermaster scenes use falling rain and an upstream wake. The pictures do not record specific damage or targets.
- **Cave, Temple and Idol.** The fallen rocks retain the Cave mouth and roots. The Temple marker retains the staircase and overhanging tree. The Idol is an original gold-colored figurine on a canvas bag and has its own state.
- **People.** Representative original adult portraits illustrate the three card roles. Each uses the same face and clothing for present, saved and killed. Saved images show shelter. Killed images use stillness without visible injuries. Left play dims the present photograph.
