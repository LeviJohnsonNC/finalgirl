# Mort investigation photography

The gallery uses original generated photographs of adults at an after-hours tabletop convention. No printed component images ship with the gallery. The neutral exposures use practical cosplay and a common backdrop. The paired Finale frame preserves a costume cue and adds the same anonymous ceramic-mask motif, with the subject caught in deeper light.

- `banner` is a 1350px-wide 3:2 hall photograph with negative space to the left.
- The ten bare suspect IDs use the left exposures of ten individually generated diptychs. The WebPs are 530px wide. The right exposures use the matching `-finale` IDs and remain roughly 760px wide for the larger panel. The crop removes the edge at the seam. Shared names and UI state remain HTML.
- The ten `-active` IDs are separate photographs based on the neutral lineup exposures. The same costumes and faces are captured closer, with tense posture and warmer side light. A temporary Active Killer uses this portrait until the token returns or the observation is undone. The crop keeps headpieces and eyes readable in both layouts.
- Dark Power and Minor assets use separate 900px-wide 3:2 stills. Anonymous Terror uses the abandoned mask, Relentless Paranoia uses the reflection, Toxic Whispers uses the registration desk, and Snitches Get Stitches uses the overturned chair. The three Minor scenes use an audience at the door, a small group of trailing guests, and a curtain in motion.
- The lineup uses consistent framing at its normal 5×2 size and tighter framing in the phone view. Krampus and Geppetto use a higher phone focal point to preserve the horns and hat. Finales share a side-lit negative-space layout on laptop and a lower text scrim on phone. Photos are illustrative when Mort is paired with other locations.

The source IDs, reviewed WebP crops, native dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Existing Final Girl trusted references and the primary Mort casting portrait keep their current app roles.
