# Razorface Play photographs

Original 1980s practical-set stills for the companion. The published board and card photos supplied rules evidence. The app displays its own artwork.

| Affliction | Afflicted | Purged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experience Unbound | Locked shackle at a closed iron gate | Broken shackle at an open gate |
| The Gift of Pain | Closed offering box under a red light | Open box with the red light gone |
| Explorers of the Flesh | Medical lamp behind green vinyl | Open curtain with daylight behind it |
| A Waste of Good Suffering | Footprints ending at a closed exit | Open exit with footprints leading outside |
| A Delicious Sacrifice | Brass hourglass by candlelight | Extinguished candle with daylight on the hourglass |
| You Call, I Come | Hanging rotary handset and dark doorway | Replaced handset and a lit hallway |
| A Lamb to the Slaughter | Hanging coat by a cold doorway | Folded coat by a warm doorway |
| Torturous Embrace | Padded chair with taut straps | Padded chair with loose straps |

The approved art direction uses named props, tactile materials and hard motivated light. Each face is 900×600 WebP and has a centered crop. The seven Power and Finale scenes and two Minor Power scenes have 320px-wide derivatives for compact choices. The module banner uses a companion-only chain and furnace scene. The existing Razorface casting portrait remains available in Casting Room. Props and photography establish no game actions.

Each pair shows the same set before and after the purge. The original Experience Unbound still is its Purged face. Once the last Affliction is cleared, a separate daylight gate scene marks the objective complete.

The [manifest](manifest.json) records the generated inputs, dimensions and hashes for the optimized outputs. The Razorface browser journeys exercise both faces of every Affliction.
