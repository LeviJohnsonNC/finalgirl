# Wingard Cottage photographs

31 original generated stills for the Play location panel and its death references. The visual target is a candid 1980s consumer 35mm photograph with believable timber, lake light and household objects. The existing Wingard casting photo informed the cottage exterior. The approved Inkanyamba photo was the reference for realism. The location photos depict no selected killer or Final Girl.

[Manifest](manifest.json) records the source filenames, generation directions, reference relationships, hashes, native and derivative dimensions, byte sizes and focal positions. The panorama is 1500 × 500. Each wide WebP is at most 160 KiB and each tile is at most 35 KiB. All exports use a proportional crop or resize within native resolution. The crop for the wide Play banner keeps the cottage right of center.
