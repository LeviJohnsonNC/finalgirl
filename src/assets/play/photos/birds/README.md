# Terror From Above photography

Original generated 35 mm horror photography, cropped and encoded for the companion.
The standard 16:9 Power, Finale, and Minor heroes are 1040 × 585 WebP files.
The 1500 × 518 banner uses a 57% horizontal and 51% vertical focal point for the
short Play header. The Collection cover and Melanie identity assets are pre-existing.

| Asset | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| `banner.webp` | Crows on utility lines under an overcast sky |
| `run-for-your-life.webp` | Two figures heading away as a flock rises above the street |
| `birdnado.webp` | Dense crows spiraling overhead |
| `waiting-to-strike.webp` | A crow on the railing at an empty playground |
| `insurmountable-odds.webp` | A flock seen from under a porch |
| `endless-swarm.webp` | Crows massing along a country lane and fence |
| `fowl-agression.webp` | Crows diving past a doorway at dusk |
| `bird-wall.webp` | Crows crowding an open gate and path |

The scenes evoke the card effects. People and places in the art do not establish
the active cast, Location, placement, harm, or rescue. Asset and card IDs stay
stable if crops are adjusted.
