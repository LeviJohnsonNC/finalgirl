# Hellscape photographs

Original generated production stills in the app’s candid 1980s photographic style. The photographed board and Event cards informed the stone setting and meeple colors. The three portraits portray invented people and their appearances carry zero game facts.

| File | Subject and artwork direction | WebP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `banner.webp` | The Nexus at eye level, worn stone stairs and an iron gate lit by a red practical. Center the archways in the shallow Play and Rules headers. | 1280 × 720 |
| `peon.webp` | An older man in an ivory work shirt beside a damp grille. | 748 × 499 |
| `wretch.webp` | A woman in a washed blue jacket watching from the edge of a passage. | 748 × 499 |
| `seeker.webp` | A man in a rust-orange jacket lit by a light down the corridor. | 748 × 499 |

Each role has an in-play photograph and a dedicated killed photograph. Killed keeps the portrait in the roster with the same 16:9 frame. Undo restores the prior recorded state and portrait.
