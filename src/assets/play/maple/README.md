# Maple Lane artwork

Original generated illustrations created for this import, 2026-10-24. No source
card photograph, rules, labels or interactive state is baked into these assets.
Publisher component art/credits and photo evidence are in
[the import record](../../../../docs/imports/maple-lane.md).

- `banner.webp`: 1996×788, suburban crossroads at night. Dedicated location header;
  clear-road crops also represent no Roadblock / absent or departed Cop Car.
- `scenes.webp`: 1448×1086. Four columns, three rows, 362×362 cells, row-major.
  Cells 0–3: NW house, NE/Boyfriend house, SW/Smalley house, SE house.
  Cells 4–7: burning house, Roadblock, Cop Car, Bicycle.
  Cells 8–11: active Fireworks, spent Fireworks, armed Booby Trap, triggered trap.
  The four house views are quadrant motifs, not a claim that their architectural
  details are a literal board reconstruction. HTML identifies each of 12 slots.
- `people.webp`: 1536×1536, three columns and three rows, 512×512 cells.
  Columns: Boyfriend, Smalley 1, Smalley 2. Rows: present/in danger, saved/relieved,
  killed/empty aftermath. The three illustrated people are fictional adults;
  clothes/faces are visual interpretation, not new canonical character facts.
  The generator's unequal row boundaries were individually cropped (source
  x: 0/418/838/1254; y: 0/412/807/1254), fitted without stretching into equal square
  cells, and assembled in identity order before display. Do not sort this mapping.

Unknown and removed portraits are explicitly labeled and subdued. Search X markers
are HTML over the house image; a searched house stays a house. Fire/Trap/search
are independent facts. Cleared fire uses an unburned house. Spent Fireworks and
triggered traps have their own physical aftermath paintings, not darkened active art.

All three originals and the normalized portrait cells were visually inspected
before integration. Browser screenshot verification and final results belong in the
import record; artwork inspection alone does not establish UI quality or rules accuracy.
