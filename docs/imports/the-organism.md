Revised planning draft, November 11, 2026. Verified GitHub `main` at `40e81db46c4b7eca5db47ed5d7b6303ae19292d9`, which includes the Slayer mirror playtest follow-up. The [approved Groves standard](../MODULE_STANDARD.md), [rendered references](../reference/module-standard/README.md), [shared controls](../module-controls.md), [import playbook](../MODULE_IMPORT_PLAYBOOK.md), and the [Intruders](the-intruders.md) and [Slayer](slayer.md) records guide this plan.

Extend the existing **The Organism** killer in **Panic at Station 2891** (`s2-panic-station-2891`) using killer module ID `organism`. Scope covers standard killer setup, Rules, Play, death, opening, ending and historical compatibility across locations. The existing film, Kate and Uki, casting and Collection art, Station loader and location references already exist. The Station 2891 helicopter, Frostbite and other location mechanics are outside this killer import. No optional mode or primary Dark Power is evidenced.

Current app gaps include a generic 10-HP killer, fallback Horror 4, one name-only killer Terror entry, and a description that treats the torso-maw creature as the only form. `killerSpecialRules.ts` incorrectly presents destroying a single core and Horror above 6 as general rules. The existing Finish loss choice already records assimilation versus being caught. Preserve its historical output and feed new, verified observations into the current ending flow.

## Verified mechanics and rules corrections

- Start with three Exposed Victims (red, black, grey), no revealed killer, Horror 5 and Bloodlust 0. Eight visible Bloodlust positions read 0–7 from the starting circle through the highest row. The Lab is the printed Lab at Station 2891 and the setup card’s killer space elsewhere. One Test Kit sits at each Item deck, commonly three. Exposed Victims always panic during Panic, cannot be saved or replaced by Special Victims, and are ineligible targets for Mutated Organisms. Other effects can force an immediate test.
- Record the physical result. A normal **Safe** result removes that Test Results card and returns the figure to ordinary-victim rules. A **Safe** result from an effect that killed the victim instead confirms a normal casualty and the applicable Bloodlust increase. **Assimilated** reveals the next face from the right, associates the meeple color with that specific form, and applies its Bloodlust entry effects from the lowest row through the current row. The physical Assimilated result goes back into the deck. **Are We Alone?** is a card-specific exception: its Safe result keeps that person Exposed and returns the result to the deck. Respect its stop-on-Assimilated timing in Rules.
- Mutations are killers and can coexist. Resolve them left-to-right on the physical board, with the F action immediately after the K action when the Finale is active. Encode each form’s attack, move, entry effects, K/F actions and shared overflow in Rules. A reveal checklist shows the relevant effects in bottom-up order. Follow the booklet’s Tough example at Bloodlust 3, where the first movement uses its current Move 2. The board determines actual movement, targets and modifiers. With multiple forms, the overflow discard is shared. Victory requires resolving the exposure and defeating all mutations.
- Use the [core Final Health procedure](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-asmodeeca/uploads/2025/10/Final-Girl-Rules.pdf) together with the killer's phase-end exception. Zero normal Health can coexist with an unspent token. When Final Health is actually lost and another mutation remains on the board, record the pending check. Blank or spent white tokens mean defeat when resolved. A successful black-token reveal records the reported recovery and replacement white token. The +1 die benefit applies once across all qualifying mutations. It is derived from the observed Health and token state, without a separate toggle.
- **Acidic Body Fluids** (1 heart), **Extra Legs** (2) and **Thick Skin** (3) belong to the closest applicable mutation or wait for the next reveal if none is in play. Allow all three on one form and retain their separate health pools. The Acidic heart cannot be lost until the form's other normal Health is gone. Extra Legs adds one to Movement. A power draw and its assigned owner are separate from damage or turn resolution.
- **Reductive**, **Cognitive** and **Regenerative Assimilation** all assimilate remaining Exposed Victims when revealed. Reductive causes a loss if the Final Girl shares the sole mutation’s space at the start of Killer phase. Cognitive causes a loss if she cannot discard two Action cards at that point while one mutation remains. Regenerative has a two-Horror start-of-phase effect while one mutation remains and a separate loss sentence if Horror is ever above 6 while that Finale applies. Preserve the distinct causes in Rules and Finish.
