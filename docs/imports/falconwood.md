## What already works

- Casting already offers the five mission names, a random draw and a premise. It currently clears the scenario selections after a location change and allows Play when the three cast slots are filled. Require a valid Falconwood mission for a fresh start and validate it at `Index.handleStartGame` as well as the picker. Scope mission context to Falconwood on launch and at prompt boundaries. Preserve the existing saved name and map it to a stable internal mission ID. Use the existing manual or random selection. A resumed game keeps its saved mission or an explicitly unrecorded one. Record a known legacy mission without redrawing or resetting the game.
- Rules already covers the broad location sheet. Setup and Event descriptions in `featureFilmDetails.ts` are introductory. Extend these with verified mechanics, searchable mission steps and rewards, both token references, exact setup placements, card effects and owner-scoped suggestions. Reuse Collection, loader and casting art. Replace conflicting generic cul-de-sac and always-present supernatural or vehicle details in prompt guidance with a river town and the actual selected context.
- The location has no illustrated Play module or durable mission observations. `GameOutcomeForm` already accepts Completed/Incomplete plus free-text mission progress or Friend and appends those fields to highlights. `TheEnd.tsx` sends the mission name and premise separately to prose and poster. Replace the new-session questionnaire with a compact Play-prefilled review, keeping existing legacy text and archived stories intact. A typed snapshot supplies both ending requests. Existing history persists `game_highlights` and `play_events`, with no mission column. Keep typed review data in the active ending checkpoint, preserve accepted Play facts in the log, and render one reviewed summary into history highlights. Avoid parsing old highlights into gameplay facts.
- Use the approved Groves frames, Sunnydaze’s direct action and ally patterns, Wingard’s stable large person and item cards, and Slayer’s separate ownership and historical death context. Check the source-mapped controls in the rendered app before producing the full art set.

## Play design

Below the usual four trackers and the killer panel, Falconwood uses one location header. The collapsed summary shows the mission and a short next-action or completion status. Expanded Play leads with one wide illustrated mission card. Its controls sit below the photo, with a discreet Rules link. Show the selected mission alone. A mission-specific card can say **2 of 3 keys**, **Deliver to Police Station**, or **Waiting for Survive the Hunt**. Use a responsive grid for relevant people, mission rewards and Events, normally two columns on laptop and one on narrow phones. Card dimensions stay stable through state changes.

**The board remains the source of truth.** Record physical observations with direct controls and quiet corrections. Leave token draws, Time, die rolls, cards discarded, positions, movement, agent targeting, combat and ordinary item inventory on the table. Use numeric strips for observed counts and clickable slots for observed clue values. A successful physical completion is explicit, using the mission-specific action below. Track only the current facts useful for the next decision. A physical result can be recorded even if prior steps were played off-app. No new generic mission framework is needed.

| Mission | What Play records and how it completes | Gain and lose progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Save Your Father** | Banked keys against the current requirement, normally three. The count describes keys retained on the sheet after a Police Station attempt. **Record father freed** confirms completion, puts Adopted Father in play and surfaces the one-Item reward in Rules. The second blank ends the Action phase and all tokens revealed that attempt return to the pool. Stopping sooner banks the revealed keys and removes the revealed blanks. The app needs the banked result only. | Record the actual lower or higher requirement from the sheet. Keep the threshold correction reachable without tracking each draw. |
| **Crack the Code** | Six selectable known token values and their sum. The Shopping Mall attempt uses the physical d20 plus the sum against 20. Show the needed roll when the token sum is known. **Record code cracked** confirms the actual successful attempt and brings in Jockette. Rules covers the Shopping Mall Item reward. | Add the revealed value. Remove the highest recorded value when the player confirms the printed loss effect and the recorded set is complete. If only partially recorded, let the player correct the observed set. The board handles farthest-space placement. |
| **Expose the Lab** | Recovered evidence versus the current requirement and an optional single revealed lead. Resolve token 1–6 as Best Friend’s House, School, Library, Fanghorn Forest, Community Pool and Town Fair, in that order. Recovering evidence clears the lead and can record the next observed lead in the same action. Default requirement is three. At target show **Deliver to Police Station**. **Record evidence delivered** completes and brings in Reporter when recorded as part of that handoff. The two chosen Items stay on the table unless individually recorded. | Record the physical requirement. The printed loss adds a fourth token to the Lab and requires an additional handoff token. Hidden token selection stays at the table. |
| **Missing Friend** | One observed value per A, B and C clue. Duplicate letters leave the recorded clue intact. Once all three are known, display the mapped destination below. Distinguish clues solved, Friend found prone and **Record friend revived**. Revival completes and grants the Best Friend and Spell Book when recorded with those rewards. Rules covers the Horror reset and health-recovery card requirement. | Record the chosen revealed clue or discard a duplicate at the table. Remove the actual returned clue on loss. Correcting clues after a physical placement preserves the observed placement until separately corrected. |
| **Survive the Hunt** | **Waiting for Terror** precedes an active hunt or zero Agents remaining. During physical setup add its conditional Terror among the first eight after building the deck with one fewer card. The printed effect starts the hunt even when discarded by another effect. **Start hunt** records the resolved trigger and observed starting count. Show Agents remaining and an Attack 3 indicator only when observed. **Record hunt survived** confirms that all Agents were defeated. A confirmed live resolution brings in Kind Scientist and Telekinesis. Rules covers starting locations, action order, panic, 3 Health and 2 Horror reduction. | Before the trigger record the three-Agent start modifier if applied. After the trigger record an Agent defeated. Losing progress sets Attack to 3. Track the count and modifier, with removal choice and locations on the board. |

Use one explicit completion action. When the next observed action also completes the mission, let that action record the final progress and completion together, with one Undo. Recording the live physical resolution can include the printed mandatory Friend and Mission Item placement in the same observation. That establishes availability at the printed space, never co-location, a discretionary Item pick, or actual use. An off-app attestation establishes completion alone and can be recorded with no clue/count history. Correcting a historical completion, using a reward and changing a Friend outcome are separate operations. Repeating or correcting completion cannot reset spent charges or a later Friend outcome. Keep Health, Horror and victim totals on the existing controls. Surface any immediate reward once in the completion action or its Rules link.

### Supporting state

- **Friends.** Show only the mission’s Friend when relevant. Best Friend has an explicit prone state with dedicated art before revival. Other mission Friends enter at completion. Reuse shared character outcomes and quiet correction for legal Saved, Killed and Left play states. Active portraits are vivid. Left play is grey and clicking it restores In play where legal. Saved and Killed have distinct photographs. Leaving play never undoes a completed mission or automatically changes ordinary victim totals. Document following into the Killer’s space, target priority, the no-panic condition, and role-specific benefits in Rules.
- **Mission Items.** A large Spell Book card records an observed spend. Telekinesis has two charges with a direct use control and quiet correction, including zero. Both only appear when relevant to the selected mission and known earned or explicitly recorded. These observations never resolve damage or automatically change Health.
- **River and Events.** Keep a compact Events group below the mission. Record Dry Spell, Packed Bridge and Freaks and Geeks via shared picture toggles. Record Van with you or left behind and whether Grand Opening and Bridge Repair were resolved. The exact altered bridge and route choices stay on the physical board, so added bridge and closed bridge observations can coexist. Use full-size state portraits for Conspiracy Theorist and Head Researcher, including distinct outcomes. One-shot Game of Chance stays in Rules and existing event recording. Helping Hands and the selected loss branch of This Indecision’s Bugging Me use the mission’s actual progress controls. Merely selecting either card never edits progress. Keep Pool Noodle and Jockette crossing permissions in Rules. A blue River Crossing is different from a road bridge, is normally inaccessible, and never counts as adjacent. A placed Bridge token changes the chosen connection. Ordinary river crossing rules do not override Slayer's separate dimension rules.
- **Fresh and resumed.** Seed verified values via existing first-write setup provenance. In particular, a selected starting Helping Hands does not identify a revealed clue, chosen Agent or banked key. Seed deterministic persistent Event facts only when guaranteed by setup and keep decisions explicit. Old sessions with a mission name and no progress log start unrecorded. Old sessions without a mission can record a known physical mission without erasing unrelated events, or keep its identity unknown. Keep unknown, known zero, awaiting trigger and resolved distinct. Render and expansion are read-only.

### Final Health and narrative

Falconwood protects the last remaining Killer from damage that would reveal their Final Health token before mission completion. Show the source-backed condition in the mission status when relevant. Health remains a manual observation. Keep the generic victory requirement separate from damage and character-specific conditions. Poltergeist still uses its rescue objective, and Falconwood uses its mission confirmation without displaying a health gate for Poltergeist. Intruders’ down, pending and killed states cannot make the app infer which physical damage was legal. No publisher clarification of unusual mixed-cast edge cases was found in this review.

**One review across Finish paths.** Resolve existing forced-loss rules first. On a new Falconwood Won transition, use the accepted Play completion or an explicit board confirmation in the existing Finish flow before `Index.handleGameEnd` calls `recordGame`. Persist that confirmation to the current case before proceeding and reuse it in the review. A player can return to Play or select Lost. Existing incomplete or unknown tracked progress is historical evidence and can be superseded by a confirmed off-app completion at finish. If a legacy session has no saved mission name, record the result as confirmed with mission identity unrecorded, with no invented details. Loss can have a complete, incomplete or unknown mission. A later Lost-to-Won review uses the same rule. Both **Generate ending** and **Save record only** validate against the latest accepted state at `saveReviewedResult` before saving or queuing generation. A failed save leaves the same case and confirmation retryable. Preserve prior results, draft text and stories. Never use Finish to grant a Friend or Item retroactively.

Store owner-scoped, mission-validated observations in the existing Play log. Keep one typed review in the active ending checkpoint and include its effective status in existing ending revision comparison. For old drafts, retain entered mission notes as notes and use an explicitly entered completion status where valid. Write one human-readable review summary into existing history highlights. Use the same typed context for prose and poster with identical precedence over any earlier progress. Accept legacy requests with no new review field and keep cached results intact. Opening uses the unresolved premise only. For deaths, derive relevant Falconwood context from the historical log prefix, keep other owners in budget and preserve pre-import signatures, cached takes and narration. An Agent can be an attacker. Its removal does not add a Victim kill. A conditional card can supply context if its mission is selected, while selecting a card never establishes an actual death.

## Mission completion follow-up

Banking the required keys and recording the final Agent defeated complete the mission in the same accepted observation. Entering a successful physical d20 result completes Crack the Code. Expose the Lab and Missing Friend still distinguish collecting evidence from delivering it and finding the Friend from reviving them. The Complete mission button accepts a physical-board result when Play progress is incomplete. It does not infer unobserved rewards or grant Health or Horror changes. Previous snapshots keep their original replay behavior.
