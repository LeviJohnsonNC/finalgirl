## What belongs in Play

Lead with a wide photograph of the **Huey in the Clearing**, followed by the **Soldiers**. Below them, show what the game has actually brought into play. An ordinary round can pass without touching the location panel.

| At the table | What the player does in Play | What the app remembers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Final Girl reaches the Huey with Victims | Tap **Evacuate**. In an ordinary rescue, enter only the newly counted saves and confirm. **Record departure only** handles rescues already entered. For a cast with special rescue handling, use its established rescue controls and record departure only. | One ordinary evacuation writes the new saves, departure and end of an active *Get to the Choppa!* together. Departure only writes the Huey and Event changes together, without touching any prior saves or portraits. A save at another Exit leaves the Huey in place. The Event returns an absent Huey and activates its per-Victim rewards even if the Huey was already there. Resolve eligibility, rewards and applicable Soldier costs at the table. |
| Train or a card flips an upgrade | Tap the matching token in **Soldiers**. Keep the two Move and two Attack rows recognizable, including which started unlocked. Show the four abilities by their printed meaning. | Each of the eight faces is independent. One Move and one Attack start unlocked. Both can subsequently be locked. Saving Soldiers changes the cost per Soldier from 2 Time to free, Range adds adjacent spaces, Damage changes half the group from rounded down to rounded up, and No Friendly Fire removes the roll. For example, three Soldiers deal 1 damage before the Damage upgrade and 2 after. |
| Twitchy Soldiers is drawn | Activate its picture. The Soldier panel immediately reflects the wider friendly-fire range. Later, unlock No Friendly Fire in the usual place. | The Event locks No Friendly Fire if already unlocked, makes 1–3 dangerous instead of 1–2, and ends when that upgrade is unlocked again. Record each linked change together. Low Morale and a failed Train only lock a token when the player actually resolves that effect and chooses a token. |
| The terrain changes | Resolve **Cave Collapse** to change the Cave photo and remove its Exit. For **Piranhas!**, pick Lower, Central or Upper River after the physical roll. For **Cursed Temple**, tap the active scene when the curse is spent. Heavy Rains uses the shared ongoing Event picture. | Cave access, river hazard location, Temple token and weather are distinct. The Cave remains a playable space after collapse. The Clearing, Cave and northwest Exit are independent. A drawn card can be known while a placement is still unknown. Entering a space, health loss and casualties require observations at the table. |
| A named person enters or leaves | In an eligible cast, a starting or later Event reveals one portrait for **Sgt. Weathers**, the **Archeologist** or the **Rebel Leader**. Use Saved, Killed and Left play. Restoring Left play reactivates that same person's record. | Weathers counts as a Soldier. His card grants him and the Victims with him the printed order bonuses. A newly resolved death calls for two Horror increases. Preview their effect, apply a known in-range change once with the outcome, and leave unknown levels or overflow for explicit tabletop resolution through the existing trackers. A status correction never reapplies Horror. The other two people cannot receive Soldier orders. Derive each named Event’s presence from its person record. Portrait outcomes and shared victim totals remain separate. |
| Rivermaster is revealed | In standard play the small choice opens into a wide river photograph with two shared health hearts. The cast-specific treatments below use the same card identity. | Standard zero, restore and unknown states follow the shared Minor control. Mort’s disguise and the Podarchs each change how the card is discarded. Apply the selected killer’s rule when selecting its controls and describing its effect. |

**Tabletop boundary.** Keep the four Action cubes on the printed Soldiers card. The group already has to choose its space, place a cube on a free unlocked row and move cubes during Upkeep. Duplicating that sequence would make routine orders the busiest part of the app. Label digital rows **Unlocked** or **Locked**. Their availability this turn is visible on the table. Keep movement, group sizes, rolls, friendly-fire results, Time, ammo, equipment and deck order physical. The MRE’s cube advance, Cigar training die and Walkie-Talkies movement bonus remain searchable. Players can still record an actual death or an Item find through the usual entry points.

**One Item earns a place in Play.** A faceup **Cursed Idol** prevents the Final Girl from resolving Search cards anywhere. Record its presence on a picture of the idol. Taking it does not clear the restriction. After actually discarding it at the Temple, clear it with the same control. A historical Item find alone never activates it. The independent curse token at the Temple can coexist with the Idol. In contrast, Supply Drop already puts the three available choices faceup beside the Supply Depot pile. Its setup and expiry belong in Rules alongside Rebel Raiding Party and instant Terrors. The existing **Other moment** is available to someone who wants to remember a draw. Play has no general-purpose Event/Terror draw log. Keep the Booby Trap and River Mud’s markers physical.

**Finding an effect.** Under the Huey and Soldiers, use a compact disclosure for inactive eligible people, effects and the Minor. The picture for an Event adds it directly or asks only for its required placement. Keep recorded states easy to find, including saved/killed portraits. Starting Events and manual entry feed the same observation. The Huey owns *Get to the Choppa!* and the squad owns Twitchy Soldiers. Supply Drop or an instant Terror never adds an empty tracker. A fresh, quiet panel needs no open grid of unused photographs.

## Implementation record

The implementation lives on `codex/pesadilla-verde`, based on the local Volk’ar revision `5ed37eb`. Casting offers the five printed Setup names and eligible starting Events. Rules uses original summaries and a publisher booklet link. Initial figure placements come from the physical Setup cards. This companion uses original location photographs and role portraits. The reference set does not include a verified official likeness for the named people.

- The Huey group write adds only newly counted Victims. A decrease through the shared counter corrects the passenger count, including its last counted Victim, while preserving the recorded departure. Use timeline Undo to reverse both together. A later Huey return must be undone before reversing an earlier dependent departure.
- Fresh setup determines the Huey, Cave, Soldier upgrades and what the selected starting Event establishes. A faceup Cursed Idol can come from the physical starting Item piles, so its initial state is unrecorded. Recovered unknown training faces allow either a locked or an unlocked observation directly.
- The separate save route preserves the selected killer’s own linked outcome. A Weathers death can apply the known ordinary Horror increase without touching victim totals. When the value is unknown or the increase would overflow, the prompt requests a tabletop resolution and an explicit tracker update.
- Rivermaster reuses the existing killer rules for Podarch rescue/Finale cleanup, Mort’s disguise, the Organism’s mutation assignment and the Poltergeist’s permanent Minor removal. Razorface’s Afflictions gate activation. Where the Birds rules leave its disposition open, Rules remains the reference and Play supplies no killer target.
- The location reuses its selected Casting identity and original Cover preferences. No database schema change or new private opening reference is required by the current diff. Narration and death generation consume the shared historical observations and require compatible bundles before the frontend release. The new opening guidance is supplied through the existing frontend context.
