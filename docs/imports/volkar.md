# The Green Terror — implementation plan

Revised November 27, 2026 against application [revision f7b19c9](https://github.com/LeviJohnsonNC/finalgirl/tree/f7b19c9924073b1eb17165cb673c27a78b38436e). Scope is the Volk’ar killer and the two playable Final Girls in standard play. This workspace holds the planning record. Implementation belongs in the application repository, where this record can carry the verification evidence forward.

## The experience at the table

On opening Play, the Volk’ar’s picture looks almost empty. Refraction along the edge of a shoulder and a blade gives her away. The player builds the Path with the physical cards. When the meeple returns to the board, one tap reveals the armored hunter in the same composition. If it was a blind attack, the player can capture what happened. If it was an ordinary reveal, they can go straight back to the board.

| Tabletop moment | What the player does in Play | What the app remembers or shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set up the killer | Start a new game with the Volk’ar | 10 Killer Health, Horror 4, starting Bloodlust and Concealed. Use the printed attack progression. She has no Movement Value. |
| Her physical Path resolves, or she cloaks again | Tap the killer picture to record Visible or Concealed | Change the module art, collapsed summary and Killer Health image together. While using Track, even revealing the last facedown card can leave her Concealed. Show an explicit unrecorded state on old logs with no observation. |
| A blind attack reveals the hunter | From Concealed, optionally open **Blind attack** and choose **Hit the Volk’ar** or **Ambushed** after resolving the physical Path. A plain reveal still takes one tap. | Either choice records Visible plus the reported outcome together. The hit image shows the cloak flaring on impact. The ambush image reveals her close to the viewer. Use the normal Health and victim controls for actual losses and casualties. |
| A Dark Power, Minor or Finale enters play | Choose the physical card through the existing reveal controls | Small photographic choices give way to one wide selected scene. Support unnamed reveals, corrections, multiple Minors, health changes, removal and Undo. |
| Self-Destruct is revealed | Track the printed Reveal effect and three countdown tokens on a newly named-at-reveal Finale. Tap a charge when the physical card removes one. | Keep the precise goal next to the charges. To win, kill the Volk’ar and **be on an Exit** before the final token is removed. The order of killing her and arriving at the Exit is immaterial if both conditions hold in time. |
| A Final Girl flips her card | Use the existing Ultimate control | Its image changes to the character’s second face. Her in-game dossier shows the recorded unlock and ability. Her Health strip continues to show her physical condition. |

There is one way to record a blind-attack result. Opening or cancelling its chooser changes nothing. Confirming a result records the reveal and result in one reversible observation. A plain reveal records only visibility and leaves no pending attack to complete. If the player picked plain reveal by mistake, the existing Undo lets them enter the attack instead. The impact or ambush picture can accompany that latest observation in the same panel footprint. Keep Visible legible and return to the ordinary state art on the next visibility change. Do not attach a mandatory dialog, new attack statistic or additional input to ordinary play.

For a concealed attack, zero potential damage leaves the Path alone. Positive potential damage resolves it. Compare the final space with the targeted space to determine a hit or a wrong-space ambush. The last Movement card supplies the normal wrong-space penalty to the Final Girl, including for another attacker. On a same-space hit the last card can make both attacks simultaneous. Chameleon of Death can apply when an attack from concealment causes the killer no damage, even if the Path stays hidden. A wrong-space ambush and Merciless Ambush’s card-by-card penalties are distinct facts. Show these distinctions in Rules. An optional picture must imply neither a wound nor a casualty.

## Powers and Finales

- **Blades in the Dark, Chameleon of Death, Stress of the Unknown, Merciless Ambush (Epic).** Give each its own reveal photograph and searchable effect. Blades concerns victims adjacent to the final space. Chameleon checks whether a concealed attack actually damages her. Stress cares about the first facedown-draw icon each turn. Merciless applies Ambush Penalties across the Path. The player can consult the active card and record resulting effects with the usual controls.
- **Energy Shield, Collector, Evasion.** Keep the shared Minor group and its reveal, unknown, removed, restore and multiple-active behaviors. Collector and Evasion use fixed pools of three and two hearts. Energy Shield starts at one. The first facedown-draw icon resolved each turn adds one while the card is active. The existing heart strip uses the same maximum for reveal and adjustment. Give the shield an owner-specific count and one-step gain/loss controls within the shared frame. Show its current number without an invented printed maximum or a row that grows beyond the phone. Zero discards it. A correction restores the observed count without triggering a gain. The table handles the once-per-turn check, Collector’s Items and Evasion’s extra draw. [Minor control](https://github.com/LeviJohnsonNC/finalgirl/blob/f7b19c9924073b1eb17165cb673c27a78b38436e/src/components/play/MinorPowerControl.tsx)
- **Rampage, Guerilla Warfare, Self-Destruct, Regenerative Cloaking (Epic).** Use the shared chooser and one named photographic card through both reveal entry points. For a fresh physical reveal with the name supplied in that action, honor the printed Reveal symbol on Rampage and Self-Destruct. Complete the Path on the board. Record the linked Visible state with the reveal and, for Self-Destruct, the starting count of three. Preserve the named card’s own action line in Rules. Entering a regular turn, advancing a round or reading a card never removes a charge.
- **Naming later is a different action.** The shared chooser stores a late name as an edit to the original reveal. If an already-revealed card is later identified as Self-Destruct, show the goal and its recorded count, or an explicit unknown with a direct choice of 0–3. A name edit supplies no new evidence of current visibility or time remaining. Clearing or correcting the name hides the inapplicable objective. Returning to the same reveal restores its last observed count. Distinguish effects linked to a misidentified card from later independent observations. A correction must not overwrite a later manually observed cloak state. Test correction both immediately and after re-cloaking. Undo restores the prior linked state. [Killer card lifecycle](https://github.com/LeviJohnsonNC/finalgirl/blob/f7b19c9924073b1eb17165cb673c27a78b38436e/src/components/play/KillerCards.tsx)
- **Finish.** Known Self-Destruct uses the normal Finish/result-review path. Name the win choice for the full printed condition and use that table confirmation in the saved result. Accept a confirmed loss, including expiration or a different cause. Let a player correct the result before saving. A recorded zero is the end of the clock, and zero Killer Health may still require Final Health resolution. Neither observation settles the outcome on its own. An unknown Finale retains the ordinary manual report. Use the same confirmed facts for save-only, narrated ending and archive.
