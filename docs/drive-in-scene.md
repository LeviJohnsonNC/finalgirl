## Visual review

The artwork was generated by editing the existing drive-in background. Rejected passes had a shallow billboard-shaped aperture and inadequate phone framing. The final scene uses a taller screen, steel sections with visible depth and bracing, embedded footings, contact shadows, grass occlusion, and consistent cold lighting. A separate review caught and fixed the responsive source/coordinate race. Artificial scan lines and standalone CSS support posts have been removed.

Review opening, projected landscape, projected portrait, and resume states. Short phones and landscape viewports scroll naturally to secondary navigation. The tape action retains its 300px desktop / 260px phone cap (227px at 320px).
